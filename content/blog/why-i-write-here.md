I have kept notes for years — scattered across a work wiki, three notebooks, and a
`scratch.md` that has followed me through four laptops. Almost none of it survives
contact with time. I go looking for the thing I figured out about consumer rebalancing
in 2023 and find a half sentence and a dead link.

So: a notebook in public. The bar is deliberately low. Not "here is the definitive
guide to X", but "here is the thing I understood on Tuesday that I did not understand
on Monday".

A few things I expect to end up here:

- Small discoveries from work — the kind that are obvious in hindsight and expensive
  in the moment.
- Notes on papers and articles I actually read, rather than the ones I bookmarked.
- Build logs from things I make on weekends, most of which will not be finished.
- The occasional long piece, when an idea has been rattling around long enough to
  earn one.

Writing in public forces a standard that writing for myself does not. If I cannot
explain why a design works, I probably do not know. That is most of the point.
