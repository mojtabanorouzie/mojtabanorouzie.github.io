About

I live where structure meets expression. By day I design and ship reliable backend systems — event-driven services, data models, and APIs that hold up under load. By night I sit behind a tonbak or lift a daf, working in the same currency I use at work: pattern, timing, and tension-and-release.

A 6/8 Persian rhythm and a well-orchestrated stream of Kafka events aren't so different — both are about getting many independent voices to stay in time. Whether I'm tuning a consumer group for throughput or tuning a daf for a performance, I'm chasing the same thing: many parts moving on their own yet landing together, exactly on the beat. That instinct — for structure that breathes — is what ties my engineering and my music into a single way of thinking.
